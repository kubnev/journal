# Journal

A private money and mood journal that runs entirely in your browser.

It keeps track of three things:

- **What you own** — your accounts, savings, investments, crypto, things of value and debts, added up into your net worth. Update a balance whenever you like; every update is kept, so you can see how your net worth moves over time.
- **Where your money goes** — log what you spend in a few seconds, set budgets, and keep an eye on subscriptions and bills.
- **How you're doing** — a two-minute daily check-in (mood, energy, stress, sleep, habits, a few lines) that you can put side by side with your spending.

## Your data stays with you

There is no account, no server and no tracking. Everything you enter is stored only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

That also means clearing your browser data deletes it. Use **Settings → Export backup** now and then. Backups are encrypted with a password you choose.

The only things the app reaches out for are live prices, if you track holdings (crypto from Binance or Coinbase, stocks from Finnhub with your own free key), and exchange rates from the European Central Bank, if you use more than one currency.

## No made-up numbers

Every figure comes from what you entered. Where there isn't enough data yet, the app says so instead of guessing. A week of logged spending is enough for averages and cash runway. Two balance updates are enough for a trend line.

Interest is shown as an estimate of what your accounts earn per day, month and year. Your balances only change when you change them.

## Getting started

1. Choose **Simple** (quick, plain input) or **Pro** (more detail per entry). You can switch at any time; nothing is lost.
2. Add your accounts with today's balances.
3. Log spending as it happens. Press **N** on any page.
4. Write a check-in when you feel like it.

Press **?** on any page for help, or take the one-minute tour. The tour uses sample data that's removed when it ends. Press **H** to hide all amounts when someone's looking over your shoulder.
